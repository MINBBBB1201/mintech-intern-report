The workbook builder requires the Artifact Tool runtime described in its command-line help; the workbook itself can be opened without that authoring runtime. No fresh workbook recalculation or Excel compatibility test is claimed for this export.

Excluded categories are employment/identity evidence, company-internal or identifying material, private research/drafting notes, university-specific strategy and application prose. The English PDFs and repository title identify the Mintech internship context. Korean reports are available as Markdown. Older local report PDFs and duplicate submission folders are not uploaded alongside the reviewed edition.
